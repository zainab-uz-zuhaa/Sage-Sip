Our Story

Founded with a passion for botanical flavors and slow-crafted coffee, Sage & Sip began as a small dream to bring a peaceful tea and coffee experience to Islamabad. We blend rich, house-infused herbs like sage with premium coffee beans and ceremonial teas to create a unique sanctuary where warm conversations and delicious brews come together.

Ethical Sourcing & Quality

We care deeply about where our ingredients come from and how they reach your cup:

  • Direct-Trade Coffee Beans: Sourced directly from small-scale farmers ensuring fair wages and sustainable harvests.
  • Organic Botanical Herbs: Fresh sage, mint, and natural spices grown locally without synthetic pesticides.
  • Eco-Friendly Packaging: 100% biodegradable cups, paper straws, and recyclable takeaway materials.
